SpotOn Profit AI is now available in open beta for SpotOn restaurant clients, with general availability planned later this year. SpotOn Profit AI builds on SpotOn Profit Assist by using broader restaurant data to identify changes, explain likely causes, and recommend actions operators can consider. The Breakfast Brothers found a 5% increase in beverage spend after testing a recommendation from SpotOn Profit AI to add single-serve mimosas SpotOn will use operator feedback and accuracy testing during the beta to shape future recommendations and expand supported data inputs.
