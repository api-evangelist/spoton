Food lovers and critics have long treated the Michelin star as the gold standard of the restaurant industry, a signal that a chef is doing something worth a special trip. But most restaurant owners and chefs don't fully understand what inspectors are actually looking for, or how a French tire company became the authority on outstanding cooking worldwide. That ambiguity doesn't have to hold you back.
