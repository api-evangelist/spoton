Restaurants hired aggressively heading into summer and then pulled back. According to new federal data released today , food services and drinking places shed nearly 33,000 jobs in June after a strong May, even as the World Cup and America's 250th anniversary brought some of the heaviest tourism traffic in years. SpotOn's restaurant data tells a more nuanced story.
